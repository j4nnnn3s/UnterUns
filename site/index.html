How to play

One house. One code. Several liars.

The app handles private roles, personal tasks, kills, ghosts, timed sabotages, cooldowns, a house dashboard, global crew progress, alerts, and the meeting clock. You handle the accusations. Finish every crewmate task, or vote out the last imposter, and the crew wins.

01 / LOBBY

Create & join

Scan the host's QR, enter a name, and join with the room code. No accounts.

02 / SETUP

Configure

Set imposters, meetings, kill and sabotage cooldowns, tasks, rooms, and a dashboard device.

03 / BRIEF

Walk through together

Everyone reviews all task and sabotage locations before any roles are assigned.

04 / PLAY

Do or fake tasks

Each player gets a random list. Only crewmate completions move global progress. At 100%, the crewmates win.

05 / KILL

Die & go silent

Victims privately report their killer, wait out a silence timer, then become ghosts. Imposters share a global kill cooldown and each has their own.

06 / SABOTAGE

Race the clock

Imposters trigger room events. Crewmates repair by trust, written code, or a dedicated device left in the room.

07 / MEET

Gather, discuss, vote

Emergency meetings are limited and on cooldown. Body found can be called any time. The caller starts the clock, then votes one player out.

08 / WATCH

House dashboard

A spare phone or TV shows shared task progress, who is alive, and which sabotages are idle or counting down.

Local game server

Your laptop is the whole backend.

Install Node.js 22+, clone UnterUns, and start everything with one command. Keep the terminal open while you play.
